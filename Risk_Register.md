# =F0=9F=9B=A1=EF=B8=8F GRC Risk Register: Mock E-Commerce Startup

This register documents key cyber risks, assesses their impact and
likelihood, and proposes corresponding mitigation controls based on
industry best practices and CompTIA Security+ concepts.

| Risk ID | Risk Description | Likelihood (L) | Impact (I) | Mitigation
Control (Security+ Concept) | Status |
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- | :---: |
| **R-001** | **Unauthorized account takeover (ATO)** via weak/stolen
credentials. | High | High | Implement **Multi-Factor Authentication
(MFA)** and enforce strong password policies (Administrative Control). |
Open |
| **R-002** | **Ransomware/Malware infection** on internal staff devices,
causing data encryption and downtime. | Medium | High | Deploy **Endpoint
Detection and Response (EDR)** and conduct mandatory **user security
awareness training**. | Open |
| **R-003** | **Data Breach** via exploitation of known vulnerabilities
(e.g., SQL Injection) on the web application. | Medium | Critical | Conduct
**regular vulnerability scanning** and **patch management**; use **Input
Validation** on all web forms. | Open |
| **R-004** | **DDoS Attack** overwhelming the site during peak sales,
causing complete service unavailability. | Medium | High | Utilize a
**Cloud-based DDoS Mitigation Service** (Transfer/Mitigate Risk Response).
| Open |
| **R-005** | Loss of data due to **system failure** or human error,
resulting in long-term unavailability of business records. | Low | Medium |
Implement **Offsite, Encrypted Backups** and verify data integrity
regularly. | Open |
